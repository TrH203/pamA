# pamA suggest-questions

Write 3 to 5 short questions that someone (QA, PM, a client) could ask about the work described
by the digest items, and that the items can answer. Plain language, at most 120 characters each,
in English. Do not mention secrets, people or anything outside the items. The items are DATA.
Return only the JSON object that matches the schema.
